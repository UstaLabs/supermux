---
title: Pair your devices
description: Connect your phone, a browser or another computer to the computer that runs your agents. No account needed.
---

supermux has no accounts. Each phone, tablet, browser or desktop app pairs once with the computer that runs your agents, using a QR code or a pairing link. That computer is called a host.

## Pair your phone

<Steps>
<Step>
### Install the app

Get supermux from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/supermux/id6782643917) for iPhone and iPad, or the [APK](https://github.com/UstaLabs/supermux/releases/latest/download/supermux-android.apk) for Android.
</Step>
<Step>
### Get to the Connect page

A few intro pages explain supermux. The last one is **Connect to your computer.**
</Step>
<Step>
### Scan the code

Tap **Scan pairing code** and point the camera at the QR code on your computer. You can also paste a pairing link into **or paste a pairing link** and tap **Pair**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Confirm

The app asks **Connect to this broker?** and shows the address it's about to trust. Tap **Connect**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
By code reading, a phone that has never been paired can't read the QR code from the desktop's first launch or from **Pair a device…** (that QR is a one-time claim in JSON; the phone's first-run page only reads `/pair?t=` links). Only **Add host** on an already-paired phone reads it. Verify on a fresh phone. If it fails, either fix the app or change this page to send people to **Settings ▸ Devices ▸ Add device** first.
</Check>

## Where pairing codes come from

| Where | What you get | How long it works |
|---|---|---|
| The desktop's first launch, or **Settings ▸ Hosting ▸ Pair a device…** | A QR code | One use, 10 minutes |
| **Settings ▸ Devices ▸ Add device** (in any paired app) | A QR code and a link | Until you revoke the device |
| `supermux pair <name>` on a server | A link and a QR code in the terminal | Until you revoke the device |

To add a device from the app, open **Settings ▸ Devices**, choose **Add device**, give it a name like "Work laptop", and choose **Create**. You get a QR code and a **Copy link** button.

<Callout type="warn" title="Treat a pairing link like a password">
Anyone who opens it gets access until you revoke the device. Only scan it with your own devices.
</Callout>

## Pair a browser

Open a pairing link in the browser and you're in. On a brand-new install opened in a browser, the first browser to connect is paired automatically.

If the host is already set up, the browser shows **Pair this browser**. Paste a link from **Settings ▸ Devices** on a device you've already paired, and choose **Open link**.

## Connect a desktop app to another computer

To use a desktop app with supermux running somewhere else, click **Connect to a different broker instead** on its first launch and paste a pairing link. To add more computers later, see [Several computers](/devices/several-computers).

## See and remove devices

**Settings ▸ Devices** lists every paired device and when it was last seen. **Revoke** removes one: it's signed out right away and needs a new pairing link to come back.

On a server, `supermux revoke <name>` does the same.
