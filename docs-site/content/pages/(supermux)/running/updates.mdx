---
title: Updates
description: Keep the apps and the computer that runs your agents up to date. Your sessions keep running.
---

Two things get updated: the **app** on each of your devices, and **supermux on the computer that runs your agents**. On a desktop that hosts, the app takes care of both.

## Desktop app

When a new version is out, a card appears at the foot of the sidebar: **Update available**, with **What's new** and **Install**. **Install** downloads the new `.dmg`, `.msi` or `.deb` and opens it. On Windows the app installs it and closes itself so the files can be replaced.

To check yourself, choose **File ▸ Check for Updates…**, or open **Check for updates** in Settings.

A hosting desktop app brings its own copy of supermux, so the agents' side is updated with the app. Updating the app doesn't stop your sessions.

## Phones and browsers

- **iPhone and iPad**: through the App Store.
- **Android**: the same **Update available** card. **Install** downloads the new APK and installs it over the old one. The first time, Android asks you to allow installing apps from supermux.
- **Browser**: the web app comes from your computer, so it's always the same version as supermux there.

## A server

A server install checks for new versions every six hours, but never installs one by itself.

- **From the app**: **Settings ▸ System** shows the version and whether an update is available. Press **Update broker**.
- **From the terminal**: run `supermux update`. It downloads the new version, checks it, swaps it in and restarts the service.
- **Going back**: `supermux rollback` restores the version you had before.
- **Docker**: run `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d` in the folder with your `docker-compose.yml`.

## Stable and alpha

There are two release channels: **stable**, and **alpha** for early builds. You're on whichever channel the build you installed came from: a stable install is never offered an alpha.

## What happens to your sessions

Updating an app stops nothing: your agents run on the computer, not in the app.

When supermux itself restarts on that computer, your apps reconnect by themselves. A session that was mid-way through picks up again on your next message.
